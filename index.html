Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Newf Bot is a Telegram bot that provides group management, games, points, moderation features, AI-assisted responses, and image search functionality.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the bot may process Telegram user IDs, usernames, display names, message counts, ranks, points, join dates, group settings, game data, and moderation records. The bot may also process messages sent to its commands and features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the bot, maintain points and ranks, run games, manage group settings, enforce moderation rules, respond to requests, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. AI and External Services

When users request AI assistance, their questions may be sent to Groq to generate responses. Web search, prayer time lookups, and image search may also involve external services. These providers may process submitted information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Image Search

When a user requests an image, the bot may search external sources and retrieve image results. Search terms and requests may be sent to the relevant services to fulfill the request. Images and links may be hosted by third parties and are subject to their respective terms and policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

Bot data may be stored in a database used to operate its features. Reasonable measures are taken to protect access to the bot and its stored data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Requests

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the bot and maintain its features. Users may contact the developer to ask questions about their data or request deletion where applicable.

7. Third-Party Services

The bot relies on Telegram and may use external AI, search, hosting, database, and image services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the developer through the bot's Telegram account: @lnll0bot.
